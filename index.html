    01 · Core values

    The core values of Continia

    Four values guide how we work — with our customers, our partners and each other.

    We dig in

    • We view challenges as exciting opportunities
    • We keep up-to-date and use the latest technology
    • We develop our skills and encourage high-quality solutions
    • We’re persistent and solution-driven

    We grow together

    • We embrace and respect individual differences
    • We trust each other and value strong relations
    • We communicate respectfully
    • We give and receive constructive feedback

    Count on Continia

    • We practice a “can-do” attitude
    • We deliver what we promise in due time
    • We listen and share experiences, knowledge, and solutions
    • We’re accountable and always go the extra mile

    We work smart

    • We share our knowledge and individual strengths
    • We’re innovative and driven by automation
    • We use our common sense
    • We benefit from diversity and various skillsets
    02 · Brand story

    Who are we?

    Times are continuously changing, but you can always count on Continia. Count on us to dig in, understand your challenges, and engineer solutions — available to the international market through our local partners.

    Our intelligent solutions add significant value to people and businesses by optimizing two of the most valuable commodities: time and data.

    We get time to work for you by developing practical, reliable, easy-to-use, and price-competitive software solutions. Continia solutions automate and digitalize workflows in the backbone of most companies: the financial department.

    With more than 30 years of experience, we know that you can’t buy back time. Don’t waste it — spend it wisely.

    To us, time is everything. Our clients count on our solutions and support for their businesses to run smoothly and seamlessly every day — that’s our promise to you. We also ensure we can count on each other as colleagues and fellow Continians.

    All Continians believe that time is the most valuable asset in business and life. Ask us what we do and whether you should work with us. We’ll say: It’s about time!

    Our payoff: It’s about time.

    Use it when the message is about the value of time — our promise to customers and partners.

    How we sound

    Confident, helpful, clear and human. Not overly playful, not corporate-stiff. The throughline is reliability: you can count on us.

    “We” talk to “you”

    We speak as Continia (“we”, Continians) and address the reader directly as “you”. Warm and direct.

    Short and declarative

    Like our values: We dig in. We work smart. Say it plainly, lead with the benefit.

    Real numbers

    Use concrete, verified figures — e.g. 30+ years of experience. Never invent filler stats.

    03 · Our logo

    The Continia logo

    The logomark symbolizes two entities intersecting with each other, while also vaguely resembling the silhouette of an hourglass. It serves as a subtle nod to the value and functional features of Continia’s solutions.

    Company name

    In 2009, Celenia Software announced the sale of its transaction-based add-ons to six former colleagues. The new company was named Continia, merging the words “continue” and “Celenia” — symbolizing that Continia is a continuation of Celenia.

    Logo variants

    There are three variants of the logo. In nearly every case, use the primary logo.

    Primary

    The primary version of Continia’s visual profile. In nearly every use case, this is the logo you should use.

    Tech Blue SVG White SVG
    It’s About Time
    Primary with payoff

    Use only when the purpose is to highlight or emphasize “It’s about time.” Ask marketing for the production file.

    Secondary

    Only for highly specific cases where the logomark has been relocated elsewhere on the canvas or visual surface of the design.

    Tech Blue SVG White SVG

    Positive and negative

    Negative is used on dark and colored backgrounds. Positive is used on light and neutral backgrounds.

    Negative

    White logo on Tech Blue, photos and colored backgrounds.

    Positive

    Tech Blue logo on white and neutral backgrounds.

    Logomark only

    The hourglass mark on its own — e.g. for avatars and small spaces.

    Tech Blue White

    Respect distance

    There should always be clear space around the logo to ensure readability. The minimum distance between our logo and other elements is the “respect distance.” It is defined by the x-height of our name feature and measured starting from the “C” in the logo.

    These guidelines must always be followed to ensure a professional appearance across media and contexts.

    Please do not

    The same rules also apply to the solution logos.

    • Trim the logo.
    • Rotate or squash/stretch the logo.
    • Outline the logo.
    • Use obvious effects on the logo.
    • Use the logo on low-contrast backgrounds.
    • Fill the logo with gradients.
    • Change the spacing between the hourglass icon and “continia”.
    • Change the color to anything other than Tech Blue, Innovation Blue, Light Blue or White.

    08 · Photography

    Our photo style

    Continia’s photography is centered around our employees. The goal is to create a warm and welcoming feeling toward our partners and customers. In specific cases, we also capture scenarios that illustrate features or actions within our solutions.

    Photo checklist

    • Feature our people. Real Continians, real offices, real partners and customers.
    • Think composition. Leave room so the photo can be used in a graphical context with text.
    • Apply our LUT. Use the preferred color profile for all photos — indoor or outdoor.
    • Remove other brands. Retouch away unnecessary logos from images.

    Favorable photo composition

    When taking a photo, keep the composition in mind so it can be used correctly in a graphical context.

    Preferred LUTs

    From a post-production standpoint, use our preferred LUT (color profile) for all photos taken. We have specialized LUTs for both indoor and outdoor environments.

    Removal of other brands

    We make sure to remove unnecessary logos from images.
